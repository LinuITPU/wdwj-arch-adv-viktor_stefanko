**_SpotyFly_**

This website is for those people who want to take a trip somewhere, but do not know where yet.
The website offers avia tickets not _TO_ a specific city, but _FROM_ a specific city.
Goal: let users spontaneously decide take a trip to some country and city and buy a ticket

Only front end part is implemented.

Basic scenario: user googles their city, is redirected to the results page, chooses a flight and books it.
Advanced scenario: the user logins / registers, is redirected to the home page, googles their city, is redirected to the results page, sets up filters, chooses a flight and books it.
